Common questions

Quick answers

How do I create my first bill?

Select "Get Started" and fill in your business, customer, and item details. Your totals update automatically.

Can I save a bill for later?

Yes. Use "Save Draft" in the billing workspace, then choose "Saved Bills" whenever you want to continue.

How can I download or print a bill?

After checking your invoice preview, choose "Download PDF" or "Print" from the top action bar.
